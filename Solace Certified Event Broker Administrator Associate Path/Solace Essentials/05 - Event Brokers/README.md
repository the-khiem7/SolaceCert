### Event Brokers screen 2 of 6 - Three Deployment Options with One Platform

The screen says PubSub+ Event Brokers are a key part of the platform and support varied enterprise requirements. PubSub+ Cloud is the quick click-and-deploy messaging-as-a-service option. PubSub+ Software can run in public or private clouds, native data centers, or on a laptop. PubSub+ Appliance is for extreme capacity, high performance, low latency, small footprint, and low total cost of ownership.

### Event Brokers screen 5 of 6 - PubSub+ Event Broker: Appliance

The appliance routes events and information in dedicated hardware for capacity, performance, and robustness, with predictable operation and low TCO. High-speed FPGAs and network processors remove the need for an operating system, enabling high throughput and consistently low latency. Built-in redundancy separates data and control planes and includes field-serviceable power supplies and internal solid-state disks. Appliances can continuously replicate messages, state, queues, and configuration to standby sites for fast failover without message loss.

Two models are listed: 3530 for mid-sized businesses, and 3560 for high-volume big data, cloud, and IoT requirements. The Go to next step control is visible, so no further lower content remains on this step.
