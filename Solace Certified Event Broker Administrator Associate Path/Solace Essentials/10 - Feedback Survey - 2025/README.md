# Feedback Survey - 2025

- **Course:** Solace Essentials
- **Syllabus order:** 10


### Feedback Survey - 2025 question screen

The survey contains four questions. Required questions ask whether the learner learned what they wanted and whether the course was engaging, each with Yes/No choices, and request a 1–5 course rating. The final question, “Do you have any more feedback?”, has a free-text Answer field and is not marked required in the visible form. No answers were entered and the survey was not submitted. Leave survey is available.
