## Current exam rules and blueprint from Solace

Solace's current [Event Broker Administrator Associate certification page](https://solace.com/learn/certifications/event-broker-administrator-associate/) describes the exam as open-book and non-proctored. It lists multiple-choice questions with one correct response, multiple-response questions with two or more correct responses, and association questions. The page says the exam has 28–70 questions depending on the exam, requires at least 70% to pass, and provides results immediately after submission with a certificate of achievement.

The page warns that the exam cannot be saved and resumed. Navigating away or exiting before submitting can reset answers and count toward an attempt. It allows up to three re-attempts, with a three-day wait between re-attempts; it advises contacting `academy@solace.com` after two attempts. The current page does not state an exam time limit. Academy's course duration of 1 hour 30 minutes is a course duration and is not recorded here as an exam timer.
