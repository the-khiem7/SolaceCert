# Feedback Survey - 2025

- **Course:** Solace Event Broker Administration

## Lesson notes

### Screen 1: Survey landing page

The Academy says **“Please share your feedback about this course with us!”** The survey landing page is titled **Feedback Survey - 2025**, says it includes **4 questions**, and offers **Begin survey**. Its decorative visual depicts a form card with a check mark; the Academy provides no download control or local screenshot path, so it is described without a substitute.

**Next:** Begin the survey, record all four questions and choices, and respond to the form before leaving.

### Screen 2: Survey questions

The survey is a single page with four questions:

1. **(Required)** “Did you learn what you wanted to learn in this course?” Choices: **Yes**, **No**.
2. **(Required)** “Did the course provide content that was engaging?” Choices: **Yes**, **No**.
3. **(Required)** “How would you rate this course?” The row asks **“On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate this course?”** with options **1, 2, 3, 4, 5**.
4. **(Optional)** “Do you have any more feedback?” A text-entry area is available.

The page offers **Submit survey** and **Leave survey**. The three rating questions are required; the free-text question has no required marker. The browser view exposes the questionnaire without any course illustration.

**Next:** Answer the three required items based on the content presented, leave optional free text blank, verify the selected values, then submit.

### Screen 3: Responses selected

The selected answers are **Yes** to “Did you learn what you wanted to learn in this course?”, **Yes** to “Did the course provide content that was engaging?”, and **4** on the 1–5 course rating. The optional “Do you have any more feedback?” text field was left blank. The accessibility tree confirms both Yes checkboxes and rating 4 selected; **Submit survey** is available.

**Next:** Submit these three required responses and record the confirmation and Academy progress.

### Screen 4: Survey submitted

After submitting, the Academy displayed **“Survey completed, thanks for your contribution.”** The syllabus marks **Feedback Survey - 2025 - Content status: Completed**. The course header reports **Course in progress, 13 of 14 lessons completed**. The only incomplete syllabus item is **Guaranteed Messaging - In progress (0/1)**; all other displayed lessons, including Summary and this survey, are Completed.

**Next:** Reopen Guaranteed Messaging and inspect its saved SCORM state and any remaining completion requirement.
