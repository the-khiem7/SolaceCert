# Feedback Survey - 2025

## Academy state

Before opening the survey, Academy showed it as the final course lesson and stated: “Please share your feedback about this course with us!” The survey has four questions and a **Begin survey** control. No answers have been submitted.

## Questions (page 1 of 1)

1. **Did you learn what you wanted to learn in this course?** Required; checkbox choices: `Yes`, `No`.
2. **Did the course provide content that was engaging?** Required; checkbox choices: `Yes`, `No`.
3. **How would you rate this course?** Required; instruction: “Select one option per row”; one rating row, **On a scale of 1–5, how would you rate this course?**, with radio choices `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, and `5`.
4. **Do you have any more feedback?** Optional free-text Answer field.

The Academy displays **Submit survey**. Answers and submission have not been made because a survey response is feedback represented as the learner and must be explicitly chosen by the learner.
