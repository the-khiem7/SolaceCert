# Foundations of Event-Driven Architecture

## Course record

The Academy identifies this as mandatory English e-learning in the Solace Certified EDA Practitioner Path, with an estimated duration of approximately 3 hours. The overview says the course teaches basic event-driven architecture concepts, Solace's event-driven methodology, and how to apply these ideas to application architecture and solutions. It is described as foundational training for Solace Architect paths and does not cover deep-dive concepts.

The stated objectives are to understand the motivations and business value of event-driven design; understand event-driven architecture and event mesh concepts; understand event-driven design patterns; apply Solace's event-driven methodology to a use case; understand good topic taxonomy and topic best practices; identify event flows, events, schemas, and microservices; and model events and microservices in PubSub+ Event Portal. The target audience is architects and developers, though the course is open to anyone starting with event-driven architectures. The page names Dishant Langayan as instructor and says learners who pass the Solace Certified Event-Driven Practitioner Exam receive a digital badge within a week.
