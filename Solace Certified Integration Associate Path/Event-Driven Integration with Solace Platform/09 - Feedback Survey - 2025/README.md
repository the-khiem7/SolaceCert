# Feedback Survey - 2025

- **Syllabus order:** 09
- **Course section:** Course survey

## Notes

The survey introduction is titled “Feedback Survey - 2025” and says “This survey includes 4 questions.” After selecting Begin survey, the single-page form presents:

1. **Required:** “Did you learn what you wanted to learn in this course?” Options: Yes or No.
2. **Required:** “Did the course provide content that was engaging?” Options: Yes or No.
3. **Required:** “How would you rate this course?” Prompt: “On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate this course?” Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
4. **Optional:** “Do you have any more feedback?” Free-text answer.

A Submit survey button is available at the bottom. No answers have been selected or submitted; responses must reflect the learner's own experience.
