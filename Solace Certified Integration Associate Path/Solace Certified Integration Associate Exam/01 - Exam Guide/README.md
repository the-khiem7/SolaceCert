## Lesson launch screen


## Purpose and prerequisite

The guide describes the Solace Certified Integration Associate (SCIA) exam as intended for people who know Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) and Event-Driven Integration (EDInt) and can use that knowledge to event-enable an organization's integration infrastructure with Solace Platform. It names the Solace Certified Event Driven Architecture Practitioner (SCEDAP) exam as a prerequisite.

The guide says the certification tests understanding of EDA and EDInt concepts and their differences; integration patterns; adding event-driven capabilities to existing or legacy enterprise resources; event-driven iPaaS; Micro-Integrations; and tools that support Event-Driven Integration.

## Exam format and rules

- **Question types:** Multiple-choice questions have one correct response and one or more incorrect responses; multiple-response questions may have multiple correct responses.
- **Type:** Open-book and non-proctored.
- **Length in this PDF:** 35 questions.
- **Duration:** 120 minutes (2 hours).
- **Pass score:** 70%.
- **Language:** English.
- All questions must be attempted; the guide says there is no penalty for guessing.
- The guide says results are delivered immediately on submission with a certificate of achievement.
- Up to 3 re-attempts are allowed, with a 3-day wait between re-attempts. After the third attempt, contact academy@solace.com.

## Taking the exam and integrity

The exam is administered through Solace Academy and needs a computer and internet connection. The guide says an exam cannot be saved and resumed later; navigating away from the tab or exiting before submission can reset responses and count toward an attempt. It also says the exam must be completed individually and prohibits posting or sharing exam questions or answers in forums or online; doing so may lead to disqualification.

## Cost and validity

The PDF lists the certification path (exam plus training courses) at $500 USD and says the path is included with the Individual Digital-Only Learning Subscription for $500 USD. It recommends the one-year subscription for unlimited access to exams and courses. The certification expires two years after passing; retaking the same exam extends validity.

The Academy course overview separately lists the standalone exam at $100 USD, the path at $500 USD, and the subscription at $500 USD. The PDF does not list a standalone exam price.

## Version and content discrepancies

- The Academy course overview says the exam has **37 questions**, while this downloaded guide says **35**. Preserve both source values; resolve the difference against the current exam instructions before starting the test.
- The Academy course page labels the test lesson **SCIA Exam: v2.1-November2025**. The downloaded guide's footer says ©2022 Solace Corporation and has no visible version label. This is a version/date mismatch; it does not by itself prove the guide is obsolete.
- The Academy overview objectives include recognizing Solace's rethinking of integration and the business impact of AI, while the guide's overview emphasizes legacy enablement and EDInt fundamentals. The topic blueprint does include Integration Reimagined and AI and the Event Mesh.
- The PDF spells “Unified” as “Unifed” in one section heading and “characteristics” as “characteristiscs” in the iPaaS bullet; the notes normalize those obvious typos.
