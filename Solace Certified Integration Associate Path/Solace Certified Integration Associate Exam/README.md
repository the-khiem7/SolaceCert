# Solace Certified Integration Associate Exam

## Course record

- **Learning path:** Solace Certified Integration Associate Path, course 2 of 2.
- **Course ID:** E-YVM371.
- **Content type and language:** E-learning, English.
- **Average completion time:** 2 hours.
- **Course status at start:** Not started; 0 of 2 lessons completed.

The exam is intended for people who know Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) and Event-Driven Integration and can apply that knowledge to event-enable an organization's integration infrastructure with Solace Platform. The Academy lists the Solace Certified Event Driven Architecture Practitioner (SCEDAP) exam as a prerequisite.

The course overview says the exam checks whether candidates can recognize how Solace is re-thinking integration; distinguish Event-Driven Integration from Event-Driven Architecture; understand integration patterns, event-driven Micro-Integrations, event-driven iPaaS and broker/connector choices, supporting tools, and the business impact of AI on an event mesh.

## Exam details from the course overview

- **Format:** Multiple-choice and multiple response.
- **Type:** Open-book, non-proctored.
- **Length:** 37 questions.
- **Duration:** 120 minutes (2 hours).
- **Pass score:** 70%.
- **Language:** English.
- All questions must be attempted; the overview says there is no penalty for guessing.
- Results are delivered immediately upon submission with a certificate of achievement.
- Up to 3 re-attempts are allowed, with a 3-day wait between attempts. After the third attempt, the overview directs learners to academy@solace.com.
- **Validity:** The certification expires two years after passing; retaking the same exam extends validity.
- **Cost shown:** $100 USD for the exam; $500 USD with the certification path; $500 USD with the Individual Digital-Only Learning Subscription.
